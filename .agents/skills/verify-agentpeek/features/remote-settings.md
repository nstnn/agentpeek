# Remote View settings

Settings has a Remote View section below Sources. It holds the Remote View switch with a status line, the Connection fields (Tailscale login, Mac address, port) with explicit Save and Cancel, the Shared projects list with Add Project…, the Share session titles switch, the Share recent messages switch, a plain list of what leaves the Mac, and a "Set up Tailscale" disclosure with the owner's steps and a Copy button for the `tailscale serve` command. Remote View is off on a fresh install and its switch stays disabled until a login and Mac address are saved. AgentPeek never runs, reads or configures Tailscale; the steps are text to copy.

## Sub-features

- `remote-settings-default`: a fresh profile shows the switch off and disabled with "Add your Tailscale login and Mac address to turn on Remote View.", the default port 47613, no shared project, titles off, and opens no listening socket.
- `remote-settings-invalid`: Save with an invalid login, address or port shows the inline message under each field and saves nothing; Cancel restores the saved values.
- `remote-settings-save`: valid values save, "Saved." appears, the switch becomes available and the viewer address line shows `https://<address>/`.
- `remote-settings-on`: turning the switch on with no shared project listens on `127.0.0.1` at the saved port and says "No projects shared yet, so the page shows an empty list."; a stream's first status has `"projects":[]` (A02). Turning it off says "Off. Nothing is shared." and closes the port.
- `remote-settings-login-change`: while on, saving another Tailscale login ends an open stream with `end: access-changed` and nothing after it, refuses the old login and serves the new one with new opaque IDs.
- `remote-settings-port-in-use`: turning on while the saved port is taken by another process shows "Port <port> is already used by another app. …", leaves the switch off and tries no other port.
- `remote-settings-projects`: Add Project… lists the fixture's folder by name with its parent path; adding it shows it under Shared projects and the stream gains it; Remove takes it out of the stream at once.
- `remote-settings-titles`: Share session titles turns on and off with its confirmation line.
- `remote-settings-messages`: Share recent messages sits below Share session titles. It is off and disabled while Remote View is off, off at every launch (also after quitting with it on and after a settings load failure) and never written to `remote-view.json`. Its hint says conversation text leaves the Mac while it is on and names the limits (latest 20 prompts and replies, up to 64 KB, never full history, files, tool input or output, or paths). Turning it on gives a stream status frame `"details":true` and the sidebar button named "Remote View on · messages shared. …" (visible text: "Remote View on" over "Messages shared"); turning Remote View off turns it off.
- `remote-settings-setup`: the disclosure names Tailnet Lock, a neutral machine name, HTTPS certificates, the `tailscale serve --bg --https=443 http://127.0.0.1:<port>` command with the saved port, the Funnel warning, the disable path, and the owner's revocation actions (remove the device from the tailnet or change ACLs) with the limit that neither takes back what a device already showed; Copy puts exactly that command on the clipboard.
- `remote-settings-recovery`: failed project removal/title hiding remains effective immediately, shows a restart warning and Retry saving control across Settings close/reopen, and retries durably before cold restart.
- `remote-settings-overlap`: a pending enable remains visible after Settings reopens; a connection save takes over the start using the saved values; OFF/quit cancel pending starts.
- `remote-settings-restart-failure`: saving an occupied new port while ON retains the new connection, turns OFF, and explains both the saved values and restart recovery.
- `remote-settings-indicator`: with Settings closed, the sidebar footer shows a "Remote View on" button ("Remote View on · messages shared" while Share recent messages is on) (tooltip = the Settings status line) only while the listener runs; OFF, a failed start and quit clear it, a saved-ON relaunch shows it only after listening succeeds, and clicking it opens Settings.
- `remote-settings-alias`: while on with titles hidden, the selected shared session's detail shows "Remote: Session N" equal to the title the viewer stream carries for it; an unshared session, titles shared or OFF show no line, and a connection change remaps it together with the stream.
- `remote-settings-viewer-count`: open Settings counts viewer pages live: a stream that opens or ends changes "<k> viewer pages open." without a new snapshot.
- `remote-settings-modal`: focus stays inside Settings while moving through the new controls, and returns to the Settings button on close.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open Settings (sidebar Settings button, rail gear, AgentPeek > Settings… or Command-comma) and scroll to Remote View below Sources.
- Type in Tailscale login, Mac address and Port, then Save (or press Return in a field) or Cancel.
- Click the Remote View switch, Add Project… and a folder in its list, Remove beside a shared folder, the Share session titles switch and the Share recent messages switch.
- If a privacy or OFF save failed, click "Retry saving Remote View settings"; the warning stays until the effective settings are saved.
- Open "Set up Tailscale" and click Copy beside the command or beside the viewer address.

## Gotchas

- Settings scrolls; the Remote View section is below Sources and may need `scroll_to`-style focus (Tab) before a screenshot shows it. After scrolling to the setup steps, the Remote View switch can sit under the dialog's sticky header, right behind Close Settings; `click` reports `recentered` when it had to scroll a covered control into view. Before that guard (TASK-026), such a click closed Settings instead.
- Pending operations are polled after dialog reopen. Unsaved settings warnings come from main-process state and survive dialog dismissal. Successful retries save the entire effective configuration, including any unsaved OFF or narrower policy.
- The status line, the sidebar label and the alias follow main's pushed status (TASK-042); a viewer page opened with `curl` is counted as soon as its stream opens.
- `type` inserts at the caret: select a field's text first (`key Meta+A`) to replace the port.
- Add Project… lists only folders of sessions AgentPeek shows; the fixture has one folder.
- Copy writes to the Mac's real clipboard (there is no isolated one).
- The rate limit (20 requests burst) applies to the curl checks; space them out as in [Remote View listener](./remote-listener.md).
